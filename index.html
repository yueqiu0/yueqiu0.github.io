I am a final-year undergraduate in Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, expected to graduate in June 2027. My research interests lie in efficient LLM agents, LLM-guided tabular learning, and self-evolving agents.

I have worked with Prof. Qinbin Li on hypergraph-based agent planning and workflow reuse, as well as LLM-guided decision trees for few-shot tabular classification. Looking ahead, I am interested in organizing agents’ past experience into hierarchical, modular representations to support more effective reuse across tasks.

Research Interests

  • Tabular learning: Using LLMs to develop data-efficient methods for tabular classification.
  • Agent efficiency: Reducing redundancy to improve agent efficiency in parallel task execution.
  • Self-evolving agents: Structuring agents’ historical experience into hierarchical, modular representations for reuse across tasks.

  1. From Prompts to Trees: Effective LLM-Guided Tree Generation for Few-Shot Tabular Classification [arXiv] [Code]

  2. G-Agent: Efficient LLM Agent with Hypergraph Planning

    Jingyi Jia*, Yuetong Cheng*, Yue Qiu*, Qi Liu*, Shifeng Liu, Minyang Hu, Xiongwei Han, Xiaofei Liao, Hai Jin, Qinbin Li

    Under submission

    TL;DR

    G-Agent uses hypergraph memory to retrieve and compose workflows and planning fragments. It batches compatible requests to reduce repeated planning and LLM calls while preserving task success.

  3. VP-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Visual Prompting in Multimodal Large Language Models [arXiv]

    Mingjie Xu*, Jinpeng Chen*, Yuzhi Zhao, Jason Chun Lok Li, Yue Qiu, Zekang Du, Mengyang Wu, Pingping Zhang, Hongzheng Yang, Wenao Ma, Jiaheng Wei, Qinbin Li, Kangcheng Liu, Wenqiang Lei

    AAAI 2026

    TL;DR

    A two-stage benchmark for visual-prompt perception and downstream use, covering 30,000 prompts, eight shapes, and 355 attribute combinations. It evaluates 28 multimodal LLMs to study what affects visual-prompt understanding.
